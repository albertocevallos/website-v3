# Pye Finance

<Description> Sep 1, 2025</Description>

### In a tweet

I recently co-founded Pye, a new DeFi protocol on Solana that enables DeFi from the validator side.

### Challenge

I took a break from crypto for the last 2 years.

Last summer, my friend and former Vancouverite [Erik Ashdown](https://x.com/eash0x) reached out to me with a product idea he was tinkering with in the validator space. The idea was simple, what happens if you could sell your staking rewards in chain A for rewards in chain B?

### Solution

Erik and I got to work over the summer. We originally started the project on Ethereum. My focus was on the mechanism design and engineering, while Erik focused on early product and getting feedback from the market.
Over the course of the summer, we realized that this product as demanded much more on Solana than anywhere else. We pivoted to Solana.

[Colosseum](https://x.com/colosseum) was launching the Radar Hackathon around September 2025. We decided to participate and build a prototype of the product there.

### Results

We won 3rd place at the Radar Hackathon (DeFi category).
